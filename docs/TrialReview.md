# Trial review: 4 weekly robotics briefs

**Why this trial is running at all.** The backtest failed its 50% precision bar. You chose, after seeing the result, to run the trial on robotics only. The brief states its record every week: 1 of 19 flagged topics took off within a year, against 1% at random. That's indistinguishable from chance, because the trial was chosen on a 4-of-15 record that a date bug inflated.

So this is a test of the format, not the detector: does a small, firm weekly pick get you to act? It succeeds if you act on at least 2 of the 4 study picks.

**How to fill this in:**
- **Taste:** for each pick, rate 1–5 for "how stimulating and novel does this feel". Do it when you read the brief, before you decide anything.
- **Acted on:** within 14 days of the brief, mark "y" if you spent 2 hours or more on the pick (a researcher-pal session, notes, code, or reading the paper end to end). Otherwise mark "n".

- Picks acted on: _ of 4. Success means 2 or more.
