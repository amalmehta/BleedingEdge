What the numbers mean
Score
How fast a topic is growing, as a weighted average of log₂ growth in papers and new repos. 1 means doubling, 2 means four times. Paper growth is measured against arXiv's own growth.
Early
Growing fast while still small (under 30 bulk papers in 12 weeks) and new (fewer than 10 two years earlier).
Consensus
Growing fast with 30 or more papers in 12 weeks: the research crowd has arrived.
Bulk count
Papers in cs.RO, cs.LG, cs.AI and cs.CL whose title or abstract contains the phrase, from arXiv's bulk metadata. About 0.6 times what arXiv's own search finds; the search links are there to read the papers.
Thresholds
Every threshold is an invented starting point, unchanged from the backtest. The full method is in the spec.
